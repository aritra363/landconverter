Welcome to Indian Land Area Converter — a free, accurate, and easy-to-use online tool built for India’s regional land measurement systems. From Bigha to Acre in West Bengal, Katha to Decimal in Bihar, or Cent to Acre in Tamil Nadu — this platform supports local conversions in multiple Indian languages like Hindi, Bengali, and English.

The mission is simple: make property and land measurement simple for farmers, land buyers, and citizens across India by combining accuracy, simplicity, and accessibility.
